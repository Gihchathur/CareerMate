# CareerMate logo generation prompt

Design a distinctive, premium identity for **CareerMate**, an open-source, local-first AI-powered job search and application assistant.

Create a simple, ownable symbol that can be recognized at 16–32 pixels. Explore a custom letter **C** formed by two flowing, precise ribbons, with a subtle upward-right motion and one small four-point sparkle to suggest AI assistance and career progress. Use a restrained cyan → electric blue → violet gradient with deep navy as the anchor. The icon should be geometric, balanced, clean and memorable—not a stock briefcase, robot, magnifying glass, or generic AI brain. Avoid tiny details, glossy 3D render effects, shadows that muddy the silhouette, gradients with low contrast, and any text inside the app icon.

Deliver a true vector SVG master, transparent PNG exports at 512×512 and 192×192, a square favicon, a full “CareerMate” wordmark in both light and dark variants, and a one-colour monochrome mark. Keep geometry and proportions consistent across all variants. For the wordmark, use a modern sans-serif with “Career” in navy/white and “Mate” in the cyan-blue-violet gradient; add the tagline “YOUR AI JOB SEARCH ASSISTANT” only in full-width lockups. Present assets on neutral light and dark backgrounds, but do not bake a checkerboard or background into the transparent files.
