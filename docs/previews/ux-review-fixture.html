HOW CAREERMATE WORKS

Four steps from CV to application

  1. Upload and extractYour PDF, DOCX, or TXT is parsed locally into text.
  2. Analyze and verifyThe selected AI provider organizes facts. You review and correct the candidate profile before saving.
  3. Discover and compareSearch configured sources and compare job requirements against your evidence.
  4. Tailor and reviewGenerate a role-specific CV, inspect the preview and ATS coverage estimate, then download it for your own review.
